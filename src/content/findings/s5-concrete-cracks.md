**Urgency:** Before listing — back door crack is large enough to be a tripping hazard / buyer concern.

**Trade:** Concrete contractor (preferred) or experienced handyman.

## Scope of work

- Assess back-door slab — the gap between the entry slab and adjacent section is wide enough to be a tripping hazard. May need:
  - Polyurethane self-leveling joint sealant for crack-fill, **or**
  - Mudjacking / polyjacking if there's settling, **or**
  - Section replacement if structurally compromised.
- Hairline cracks on front walkway: rake out and seal with elastomeric concrete crack filler.
- Recommend resealing surface afterward.
