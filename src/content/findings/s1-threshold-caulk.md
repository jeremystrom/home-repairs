## Diagnosis

Inspector's arrows point to a continuous gap between the underside of the aluminum threshold and the concrete porch slab. Water can wick under here and rot the subfloor at the door sill — common failure point.

## Materials

- 1 tube exterior-grade silicone or polyurethane sealant (e.g. GE Silicone II Window & Door, or Loctite PL Premium). Color: clear or tan to match threshold.
- Painter's tape (optional, for clean line)

## Tools

- Caulk gun
- Utility knife or 5-in-1 scraper
- Rag + isopropyl alcohol
- Caulk smoothing tool or wet fingertip

## Repair steps

1. Sweep / vacuum the area free of debris.
2. Use the utility knife / scraper to remove any old crumbling caulk or sealant.
3. Wipe the gap clean with a rag dampened in alcohol; let dry completely.
4. (Optional) Tape above and below the gap for a clean line.
5. Cut the tube nozzle at a ~45° angle, opening ~1/4".
6. Run a continuous bead along the entire gap, pushing the caulk gun (not pulling) so the bead fills the gap.
7. Smooth with a wet fingertip or smoothing tool within 5 minutes.
8. Remove tape immediately; let cure 24 hours before exposing to rain.
