## Progress notes

- ✅ Garage junction boxes — covers reinstalled, bird's nest cleared.
- ⏳ Garage workshop outlet (the brown plug in the first photo) — still missing its cover plate.
- ⏳ Room-by-room walkthrough to find any other missing/cracked covers — not yet done.

## Diagnosis

Walking-around chore: do a full inspection of every outlet, switch, and visible junction box in the house and bag-and-fix as you go. The attic/crawlspace junction box (second photo) is the most important one — exposed connections + bird-nest debris is a real shock and fire hazard.

## Materials

- Box of standard single-gang outlet plates (white) — $5
- Box of standard switch plates (white) — $5
- Standard 4-square junction box cover plate (for the attic box) — $2
- Mix of metal vs plastic cover depending on box type (junction boxes often need a flat metal blank cover with center knockout)
- Long device screws (6-32 × 1") in case the box is recessed too deep for the short screws that come with cover plates

## Tools

- Phillips screwdriver
- Flashlight / headlamp (for attic and dim corners)
- Non-contact voltage tester (verify dead before working on any open junction box)

## Repair steps

1. Walk the house room by room with a notepad. Note every missing cover plate, cracked cover plate, painted-over cover (cosmetic but worth replacing).
2. **For the attic/crawlspace junction box:**
   - Kill power at the breaker that feeds it (test with voltage tester first).
   - Clear debris and any animal nesting material away.
   - Verify all wire splices inside use proper wire nuts and are tight.
   - Install a flat metal blank cover plate over the box (screws to the box's threaded holes).
3. **For garage/workshop outlet:** kill breaker, install a standard outlet cover plate.
4. **For any room outlets/switches** missing or cracked covers: just screw on a new one (no need to kill power if you're only attaching a cover plate, but err on the side of safety and kill the breaker anyway).
5. Take a photo of each fix as a record.
