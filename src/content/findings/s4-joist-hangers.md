## Bundled work

One of three deck items (S3 + S4 + D2) to quote and complete together.

## Scope of work

- Fill every nail hole on every joist hanger with Simpson SD9 / SD10 structural connector screws (preferred) or 10d × 1-1/2" hot-dipped galvanized hanger nails. **Not** roofing nails or drywall screws.
- Verify hangers are the correct size for the joist material.
- Replace any rusted or bent hangers.
