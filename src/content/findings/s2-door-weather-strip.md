## Diagnosis

The kerf-in weather strip on the door jamb is torn/shredded at the bottom and dirty/compressed higher up. Standard residential entry-door jamb seal.

## Materials

- Replacement door jamb weather strip — match what's there. Most home-center kits are "Q-Lon Replacement Door Weatherstrip" (for kerf-in jambs) or magnetic strip for steel doors. Pick up a full jamb kit (~$15–25, fits one door — covers both sides + top).
- If existing is kerf-in: the new strip just slides into the same channel — no fasteners.
- If existing is staple-on foam: small box of 5/16" staples + heavy-duty stapler.

## Tools

- Pliers / needle-nose (to pull old strip out of kerf)
- Utility knife or scissors
- Tape measure
- Stapler (only if staple-on type)

## Repair steps

1. Open the door and identify the seal type: a flexible vinyl/foam bulb sitting in a slot in the jamb = kerf-in.
2. Pull old strip out — start at the top, peel down. Pliers help.
3. Measure each side (latch jamb, hinge jamb, head jamb) and cut new strip to length with utility knife. Cut the corners at 45° miters for a clean fit.
4. Press the new strip's flange into the kerf — should snap in by hand.
5. Close the door and check for compression: bulb should touch the door all the way around without preventing latching. Trim slightly if the door drags.
