## Bundled work

Third of three deck items (S3 + S4 + D2) to quote and complete together.

## Note from walkthrough

Inspector mentioned in person that **at least one board has rot at the end** and needs full replacement — the written report does not photograph or specifically identify which board. Have the contractor walk the deck and identify all boards needing replacement before quoting.

## Scope of work

- Walk the deck, identify and flag all boards with rot, splits, or end-grain damage.
- Replace flagged boards with matching-dimension pressure-treated lumber.
- Clean all debris from board gaps (pressure wash or putty knife).
- Scrape loose paint/stain, prep surface.
- Repaint (or restain) entire deck surface with deck-rated coating to match existing color.
