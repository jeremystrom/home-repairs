## Bundled work

This is one of three deck items (S3 + S4 + D2) that should be quoted and done together by one crew — deck contractor or experienced general handyman.

**Urgency:** Before listing — buyers will inspect the deck closely, and a structural call-out kills deals.

## Scope of work

- Inspect existing ledger attachment (bolt count, spacing, type, condition).
- Add additional structural fasteners (likely Simpson SDWH structural screws or 1/2" through-bolts to band joist behind brick veneer) to meet code spacing.
- Address ledger deflection — may require sistering a new ledger or jacking deck back up to original elevation.
- Re-seat floor joists into ledger and verify joist hanger nailing (see S4).
- Confirm flashing above ledger is intact to prevent water intrusion.
